## Human-Atlas / BodyParts3D

Anatomy model assets in `ChangXi/Resources/PainModels/Anatomy/` are derived from:

- **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
- The aligned, mobile-optimized source package and conversion conventions from
  **Human-Atlas**, with adaptations and attribution documented in its
  `public/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

The included assets crop the common skin, muscular, and skeletal coordinate
space into medical regions, combine structures by display layer, simplify
geometry for iPhone, convert axes, and apply ChangXi materials. Attribution:
"BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC
Attribution 4.0 International". Anatomy graphics are for location communication
and education, not diagnosis.
